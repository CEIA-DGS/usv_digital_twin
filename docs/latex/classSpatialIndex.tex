\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
High-\/performance spatial indexing and querying engine. 

Uses R-\/\+Tree spatial trees (via Boost.\+Geometry) to perform real-\/time proximity searches (OLogN), restricted zone verification (Nav\+Mesh), and dynamic target management within the robot\textquotesingle{}s ecosystem.
