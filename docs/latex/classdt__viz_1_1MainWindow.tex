\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Main window of the visualization tool. 

This class draws the navigable free zone (Nav\+Mesh), the USV, the monitored vessels, the traveled trajectory, and the planned route.

It also visually alters targets that present an imminent collision risk based on the digital twin predictive core.
